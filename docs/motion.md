# Motion

## Index

**Summary:** the app moves a little where something just happened: a live alert drops in, the counter and a score roll up, a series cell fills, the heart and the red X pop (and the heart breaks in two when you un-favorite), the share check pops, the Feed tab's badge swells when an alert lands; a live game's dot sends out rings, the first cards of a tab wave in, a game that just ended in a loss gets a gold glint, an F1 order slides when it reshuffles, the Plus upsell breathes, the Scores switches slide a thumb and their lists slide the way you went, an odds bar and its percentage glide, a placeholder shimmers while the first load waits, and a team or player screen's big name folds into the nav bar as you scroll. It is React Native's own `Animated` on the native driver (opacity and transform only), plus the phone's reduce-motion setting, so every bit of it ships over the air with no new build. Nothing here depends on a haptic (the web version has none).

**Sections:**
- **The pieces**: `lib/motion.ts` and `components/RollingNumber.tsx`.
- **Where it moves**: each animation, its trigger and its files.
- **Rules**: what keeps it OTA-safe and quiet.

## The pieces
- `app/src/lib/motion.ts`: `reduceMotion()` (the setting, read once and kept current), `useOnChange(value, fn)` (calls `fn(prev, next)` on a change after the first render, never on mount) and `usePop(peak)` (a `scale` value and `pop(delay = 0)`: swell to `peak` in 130 ms, spring back; does nothing under reduce motion).
- Also in `motion.ts`: `usePulse(on)` and `useBreath(on)` (a looping 0 to 1 value, 1,600 ms and 4,200 ms; it runs only while `on`, its screen is the focused one and reduce motion is off, and resets to 0 when it stops; `running` says whether it moves) `useShimmer(on)` (a 1,300 ms 0 to 1 sweep, same looping rules), `useSlide(index, group)` (a `translateX` that starts 16 px off in the direction the index moved and settles in 240 ms; a change of `group` changes the index without sliding) and `useWave(tab)` (the first-load entrance of a list: the first 6 rows to mount within 400 ms of the tab's first row rise 16 px and fade in over 300 ms, 45 ms apart; once per launch per tab name; with no tab, or under reduce motion, the finished state).
- `app/src/components/Skeleton.tsx` (placeholder cards with a slanted light bar sweeping across) and `app/src/components/Segment.tsx` (a segmented control with a thumb that slides to the chosen option).
- `app/src/components/RollingNumber.tsx`: a number that slides the old text out the top and the new in from below (320 ms), only for an increase from a number. `lineHeight` is required because the box is clipped to one line. `onRoll` fires when it rolls.

## Where it moves
- **Alert lands** (`FeedCard.tsx`): a `fresh` card (the Feed passes it for an alert detected after the app opened, for a minute) drops in with a spring and a red wash fades over it. Once per alert id for the app's life (a module-level set), so scrolling it away and back never replays it. The static red `fresh` border stays the full minute.
- **Counter** (`HateWatchCounter.tsx`): the total rolls and a team's chip count pops when a Successful Hate Watch arrives. The counter shows a held copy that updates only while Settings is focused (tabs stay mounted), so the roll happens in front of you, not off screen.
- **Score** (`GameCard.tsx`, `TeamRow`): a team's score rolls up when it rises, the number swells 1.3x after 120 ms and a red wash (up to .35, 1,200 ms) flashes the row. A drop or the first value just changes.
- **Odds** (`GameCard.tsx`, `OddsRow`): the bar is a `scaleX` layer from the left and the percentage counts to the new value over 700 ms (the percentage is tweened by JS, the bar by the native driver). A move under 2 points just changes.
- **Series strip** (`SeriesStrip.tsx`): a game's mark fills in from the left when its game begins or ends (a recolour from favoriting or tracking does not replay it).
- **Heartbreak** (`FavoriteButton.tsx`, `Break`): un-favoriting (armed by the press, like the pop) splits the gold heart into two clipped halves that fall apart and fade in 600 ms.
- **Heart** (`FavoriteButton.tsx`) and **red X** (`following.tsx`, `MostHatedStar`): pop when you turn them on. A press arms a ref; the pop fires only when the state then flips, so favorites finishing loading never pop. A failed favorite change disarms it.
- **Share check** (`FeedCard.tsx`): the icon pops when it turns into the check (where the link was copied; on a phone the share sheet opens instead).
- **Live dot** (`GameCard.tsx`, `LiveDot`): two translucent rings swell from the solid green dot (scale 1 to 3, opacity .6 to 0, a 1,600 ms loop, the second half a cycle behind) on a game that is on. Only while the Scores tab is the one showing; none under reduce motion (the dot stays solid). F1 has its own body and no dot.
- **Wave in** (`GridRow.tsx` `wave`, `useWave`): the Scores and Feed lists pass a tab name, so their first rows rise in one after another the first time the tab loads in a launch. A refresh, a filter change or a row scrolled into view later just appears. The Feed skips a row holding a `fresh` alert, which has its own entrance.
- **Loss glint** (`GameCard.tsx`, `Glint`, `noteFinished`): a 28 px flat gold bar crosses the card in 750 ms (opacity up to .5 and back, 200 ms after it lands) when a game turns from on to over while you watch, and one of your sides lost. Scores sections split by game state, so the card remounts when it finishes; `noteFinished` (fed from the Scores render) remembers each game's last state and which ones just finished (10 s), and the card takes that on mount or on a state change. A game already over when the app loads, a scroll or a refresh never glints.
- **Race order** (`RaceOrder.tsx`): when the order changes the rows slide to their new places (`LayoutAnimation`, 420 ms ease-out). When exactly two neighbours swap, the driver who gained the place flashes green and the one passed red for 900 ms; a pit-stop shuffle of several rows just slides. Android's sliding is not verified (`setLayoutAnimationEnabledExperimental` is never called). The green and red are the plain good-and-bad reading of a pass, not the app's usual tracked-driver colours (there, a tracked driver losing places is hate-coloured good news).
- **Shimmer** (`Skeleton.tsx`): the Feed's first load and the UFC loading state show placeholder cards, only if the wait passes 400 ms (250 ms fade-in), with a light bar sweeping across. The Scores games list has no loading state, so none there. Still under reduce motion.
- **Sliding thumb** (`Segment.tsx`, `scores.tsx`): the Scores view switch (My, Special, All) and the F1 races switch (Live, Upcoming) slide a thumb to the chosen option, and the list under them slides in 16 px from the side you moved toward (`useSlide`). When the set of options changes (Special appears or goes, or the F1 filter is picked) the index changes without a slide.
- **Header fold** (`target/[key].tsx`): scrolling a team or player screen fades and shrinks (1 to .88) the big name over its first 48 px while the same name fades into the nav bar title. The header's hairline does not animate.
- **Badge pop** (`(tabs)/_layout.tsx`): the Feed tab's badge swells to 1.55x and springs back when the unseen count rises. It rises only on a live frame, never on load; the vendored `Badge` fades in by its own scale, which our `transform` replaces.
- **Plus glow** (`Plus.tsx`): behind the subscribe button of the upsell, two concentric translucent rings in the accent colour breathe (opacity .35 to .7, scale 1 to 1.12, 4,200 ms). Only while the upsell shows (not for members, not without a store) and Settings is focused.
- **Tab transition** (`(tabs)/_layout.tsx`): the old screen goes at once and the new one fades in with a 10 px rise over 240 ms (ease-out). It is the scene's own style (`scene()` in the layout, passed as the `sceneStyle` option, built once per tab) driven by our own value per tab (`fades`: 1 on the focused tab, 0 on the rest, timed by `GlideTabBar` when the focused tab changes), not by the navigator's per-tab `progress`: with quick taps (Settings, Tracking, Settings) the navigator's overlapping `Animated.parallel` groups stopped each other's timings and left the focused screen's progress at its inactive end, a blank screen. The navigator's `animation`/`transitionSpec` is left off on purpose: it drives each screen's native `activityState` with an Animated node, and when that node was swapped for the plain "on top" value the Feed (the first tab, always kept attached) was left inactive, so after a switch from another tab it neither scrolled nor took a tap (the tab bar still worked). Without it `activityState` is a plain value and only the focused screen is active. A 28 x 2 px red line in `GlideTabBar` (the default bar wrapped, so its look is untouched) glides along the bar's top edge to the tapped tab in 300 ms (ease-out), and the tapped icon pops 1 to 1.25 (`TabIcon`, `usePop`). Under reduce motion the fade values are set outright (the screens swap) and the line jumps (`reduceMotion()`, read when the tab changes). The wide layout (web and iPad) hides the bar for the sidebar, so only the screen transition applies there.

## Rules
- Only `Animated` from `react-native`, with `useNativeDriver: true` (opacity and transform). A glow or wash is an overlay `View` whose opacity animates. Never drive one value with both drivers. No Reanimated, gesture handler, Lottie or Skia: a new native module changes the fingerprint and stops updates reaching builds already out.
- Every trigger is a real change in the session (`useOnChange`, a fresh id, an increase), never the first render or a reload.
- Reduce motion skips everything to its end state.
- Haptics are never added for motion; the existing ones stay (`.catch(() => {})`).
- Not built: layout make-room when an alert lands, a "swept" wipe on the series strip, a shake on a card, burst dots on the heart and X, the emoji stamping onto a landing alert, the stamp slam (study #4), a flame or bell wiggle (the Feed has no bell).
