# Hate Watch: Google Play listing text

Paste into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing. Limits: app name 30, short description 80, full description 4,000 characters. The images are in `app-store/play/` (`node app-store/build-play-images.mjs`).

## App name
Hate Watch

## Short description
Real-time alerts when the teams and players you can't stand mess up.

## Full description
Know the second your rivals fail.

Hate Watch is for sports fans with rivalries. Track the players and teams you root against, and the app tells you the moment something goes wrong for them: a strikeout, an interception, a goal allowed, a lost lead, a lost game, a crash out of a race. It watches so you don't have to, sends a notification within seconds, and keeps every alert in a feed you can scroll back through.

TRACK ANYONE YOU LOVE TO HATE
Search any team or athlete and tap Track. Covers the NBA, WNBA, MLB, NFL, NHL, college football, the English Premier League, Formula 1 and UFC.

REAL-TIME ALERTS
Strikeouts, turnovers, blown leads, penalties, injuries, lost games, drops in the standings, DNFs and dozens more. Every alert type can be switched on or off for each league.

YOUR RULES, PLAYER BY PLAYER
Turn off one alert for one player and keep it for everyone else. Mute the push for someone and still see their alerts in your feed.

WATCH THEIR GAMES AS THEY HAPPEN
Live scores and win chances for every game your rivals play, plus a box score and highlights, so you can follow along without watching.

A FEED OF EVERY PAINFUL MOMENT
Every alert in one chronological feed, with video clips where they exist. Share the ones too good to keep to yourself.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
No account and no login. Quiet hours and alert sounds are in Settings. One tap in Settings deletes everything the app has stored about your device. No ads.

HATE WATCH PLUS (OPTIONAL)
Every feature above is free. Hate Watch Plus is an optional monthly subscription that supports the app and adds a gold accent colour, a gold app icon and a star to mark your most hated players and teams. Manage or cancel any time in Google Play.

Hate Watch is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or player. Scores and statistics come from public sources and may be delayed or contain errors.
