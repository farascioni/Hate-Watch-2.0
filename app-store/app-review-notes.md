2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Hate Watch is for sports fans with rivalries. Fans track the players and teams they root against, and the app alerts them the moment something goes wrong for them: a strikeout, an interception, a goal allowed, a lost game, a drop in the standings, an injury, a crash out of a race. Normally, keeping up with a rival means watching their games or checking box scores. Hate Watch does the watching and sends a push notification within seconds, and keeps every alert in a chronological feed. It covers the NBA, MLB, NFL, NHL and Formula 1.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP (no login needed)
There are no accounts and no login. On first launch the app creates an anonymous device ID; it collects no name, email or other personal information. Allowing notifications is optional.
- Search: type a player or team name (for example "Bryce Harper" or "Philadelphia Eagles"), optionally filter by league, and tap Track.
- Feed: every alert for the players and teams you track, newest first. The share icon shares an alert.
- Tracking: the bell turns off push notifications for one player or team (their alerts still appear in the feed). The gear chooses which alert types you get for just that player or team, overriding Settings. Unfollow stops tracking.
- Settings: push on/off, alert sound, quiet hours, and an on/off switch for every alert type in each league. Also Give feedback (opens Mail), Privacy policy, Support, and "Delete all my data", which permanently deletes the device's tracked players and teams, settings and feed from our server.
- Optional tips: under "Support Hate Watch" in Settings, Donate offers three consumable In-App Purchase tips. Tips unlock nothing. Every feature is free, with no ads or subscriptions.
Alerts come from live games, so a player or team only produces alerts while they are playing. To see alerts quickly, track a team (or its players) with a game in progress or scheduled today. Search includes every team in all five leagues. Game-result alerts such as "Loses a game" arrive when the game ends.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- ESPN's public sports data feeds (site.api.espn.com and sports.core.api.espn.com): rosters, schedules, live play-by-play, standings and injury reports. Only our server reads these.
- ESPN's image CDN (a.espncdn.com): the player headshots and team logos shown in the app.
- Our own server (hate-watch-api.fly.dev, hosted on Fly.io): detects alerts and stores each anonymous device's tracked players and teams, alert settings and feed.
- Expo Push Notification Service, which delivers notifications through Apple Push Notification service.
- Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit), for the optional tips.
The app uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, sign-in or AI services.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region where it is offered. All content is in English, and alert times are shown relative to the device's clock (for example "3m ago").

6. REGULATED INDUSTRIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Hate Watch is not in a regulated industry. It has no betting, gambling, odds, financial or health content.
The game information it shows (scores, play-by-play descriptions, rosters, standings and injury statuses) is factual sports data. Player headshots and team logos are loaded from ESPN's image CDN.
