2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Hate Watch is for sports fans with rivalries. Fans track the players and teams they root against, and the app sends a push notification within seconds when something goes wrong for them: a strikeout, an interception, a goal allowed, a lost game, a drop in the standings, an injury, a crash out of a race. Every alert is kept in a chronological feed. It covers basketball, baseball, football, hockey and motorsport.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP (no login needed)
There are no accounts. On first launch the app creates an anonymous device ID and collects no name, email or other personal information. Notifications are optional.
- Search: type a player or team name (for example "Bryce Harper" or "Philadelphia Eagles"), optionally filter by league, and tap Track.
- Feed: every alert for the players and teams you track, newest first. The share icon shares an alert.
- Tracking: the bell turns off push for one player or team (alerts still appear in the feed); the gear picks which alert types that player or team sends, overriding Settings. Unfollow stops tracking.
- Settings: push on/off, alert sound, quiet hours, and an on/off switch for every alert type in each league. Also Give feedback, Privacy policy, Support, "Move to a new install" (a recovery code restores tracking on a new install), and "Delete all my data", which permanently deletes the device's tracked players and teams, settings and feed from our server.
- Optional subscription: under "Support Hate Watch" in Settings, Hate Watch Plus is a $5/month auto-renewing subscription (Restore Purchases, Privacy, Terms and Manage Subscription are on the same card). Perks: a "Gold accents" switch (red accent becomes gold), a gold app icon, a star on the Tracking tab marking a "most hated" player or team (gold border on their Feed alerts), and a star on an alert that saves it to a "Saved" list. Every feature is free, with no ads.
Alerts come from live games, so a player or team only produces alerts while playing. To see alerts quickly, track a team (or its players) with a game in progress or scheduled today. Game-result alerts such as "Loses a game" arrive when the game ends.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- ESPN's public sports data feeds (site.api.espn.com and sports.core.api.espn.com): rosters, schedules, live play-by-play, standings, injuries. Only our server reads these.
- Our own server (hate-watch-api.fly.dev, hosted on Fly.io): detects alerts and stores each anonymous device's tracking, settings and feed.
- Expo Push Notification Service, delivering through Apple Push Notification service.
- Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit), for the optional Hate Watch Plus subscription.
The app uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, sign-in or AI services.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same everywhere. All content is in English, and alert times are relative to the device's clock (for example "3m ago").

6. REGULATED INDUSTRIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Hate Watch is not in a regulated industry and has no betting, gambling, odds, financial or health content.
The game information it shows (scores, play-by-play, rosters, standings, injury statuses) is factual sports data, shown as text.
