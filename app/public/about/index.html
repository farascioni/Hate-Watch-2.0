HATEWATCH

Get a push the moment the team you can't stand loses

Hate Watch tracks the teams and athletes you root against and tells you the second something goes wrong for them: a strikeout, an interception, a blown lead, a lost game, a crash out of a race.

Open Hate Watch on the web

Free. No account. Push alerts need the iPhone or Android app.

How it works

  1. Pick who you can't stand. Search any team or athlete and tap Track.
  2. Choose what you want to hear about. Alert types can be switched on or off per league and per player.
  3. Get the alert as it happens. A notification on your phone, and a feed you can scroll back through.

Rival-alert apps like OPPS and Score Losers cover a single sport. Hate Watch covers all nine, so one app handles the football team, the basketball team and the driver you can't stand.

What you get

  • Real-time alerts: strikeouts, turnovers, blown leads, lost games and more, the moment they happen.
  • Your rules, player by player: turn off one alert for one player and keep it for everyone else.
  • Follow along without watching: live scores and box scores for every game your rivals play.
  • Private by default: no account, no ads, and one tap deletes what the app has stored about your device.

Questions

Is Hate Watch free?

Yes. Every alert and feature is free, with no ads and no account. Hate Watch Plus is an optional subscription.

Which leagues does it cover?

The NFL, NBA, WNBA, MLB, NHL, college football, the English Premier League, Formula 1 and UFC.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. One tap in Settings deletes everything the app has stored about your device.

Can I use it in a browser?

Yes. The web version at hatewatch.dev works in any browser. Push notifications need the iPhone or Android app.

Where do the scores come from?

From public sources, so they can be delayed or contain errors. Hate Watch is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or player.
