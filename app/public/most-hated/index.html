The most hated teams and athletes in sports

Every team and athlete on Hate Watch has a count of how many people track them to root against. This is the top of that list, as it stood on October 19, 2026.

How the list is ranked

A "hater" is one person who tracks a team or athlete in Hate Watch so they get an alert when it goes wrong for them. The list ranks by that count, most first, and entries that are tied share a rank (the next rank is the next count down, with no gap). It counts people, not alerts, and it never shows who they are. Hate Watch is young, so the numbers are small and one new person can move a team up a place.

Why it matters

A rival is personal. Nobody is hated by everyone, and the ranking is not a verdict on who is good or bad: it only says who the people using this app chose to root against. If you want to add your own, pick a team or athlete and tap Track, and see how rival alerts work for what you get when they lose.
